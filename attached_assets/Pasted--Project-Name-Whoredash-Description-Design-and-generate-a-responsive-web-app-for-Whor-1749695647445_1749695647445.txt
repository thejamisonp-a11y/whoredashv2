📛 Project Name: Whoredash  
📝 Description:  
Design and generate a responsive web app for **Whoredash**, a premium on-demand **lifestyle companion booking** platform. The platform connects verified independent service providers with verified clients for exclusive private experiences, bookings, and scheduling. It should resemble a hybrid of **Doordash x OnlyFans x Calendly**, but designed with high-end concierge aesthetics.

---

🎯 **Goal:**  
A mobile-first web app that allows clients to browse available profiles, view bios, galleries, pricing, and instantly request bookings. Lifestyle professionals can manage their availability, profile, and earnings. Admin panel required.

---

🌐 Pages to Create (with content & components):  
1. **Landing Page**  
   - Headline: “Your Experience, Delivered Instantly”  
   - Subtext: “Discover premium lifestyle companions on demand—available near you.”  
   - CTA Buttons: [Browse Directory] [Join as Companion]  
   - 3-Step “How It Works” section  
   - Testimonials (3 short quotes)  
   - Newsletter form

2. **Browse Directory**  
   - Grid of 12 profile cards (photo, price/hr, rating, [Details] button)  
   - Filters: Location, Price, Service Category, Availability  
   - Pagination or infinite scroll

3. **Companion Profile Page**  
   - Profile image banner, name, verified badge, star rating  
   - Bio section + “What I Offer” services list  
   - Availability calendar (selectable time slots)  
   - Image gallery (lightbox)  
   - [Instant Book Now] button  
   - Review section (name, star rating, comment)

4. **Booking Flow**  
   - Step 1: Select date & time  
   - Step 2: Fill in name/email/phone  
   - Step 3: Payment placeholder (Stripe-style UI)  
   - Confirmation screen with event summary and “Add to Calendar” button

5. **Client Dashboard**  
   - Past & Upcoming bookings  
   - Saved companions  
   - Account & notification settings

6. **Companion Dashboard**  
   - Earnings summary + stats  
   - Upcoming bookings with status  
   - Edit profile: bio, pricing, availability, gallery upload  
   - Request payout button

7. **Admin Panel**  
   - Manage users & companions  
   - View bookings calendar  
   - Edit FAQs / About / Policies

8. **Static Pages**  
   - About Us  
   - FAQs  
   - Contact Form  
   - Terms of Service  
   - Privacy Policy

---
